Permitted authority

One source location

All Construction content and file metadata came from the signed-in VET statewide staffroom’s Construction Resource Library, inspected on 1 September 2026.

  • Eight complete Task 2–7/option program drafts were inspected page by page.
  • Task 1 was mapped from its Staffroom delivery-resource structure; no equivalent program draft was found.
  • The authenticated SharePoint index supplied 420 exact leaf-file paths, formats and modification dates; 5 useful images confirmed in the folder UI bring the complete direct-file registry to 425.
  • Representative Word and PowerPoint path-based links were opened and verified against their exact filenames.
  • Direct URLs contain no Teams session identifiers or volatile authentication tokens.
  • Draft, dated, duplicate, archive and controlled cues remain visible rather than being silently corrected.

Visual scope

No invented photo quota
